EmoChess turns chess practice into a calm space for emotional reflection.

Before each game, players check in with how they feel. During play, supportive prompts encourage patience, focus, and self-awareness. After a game, EmoChess helps review moves, chats, and emotion changes so players can notice patterns over time.

Features:

- Play chess with guided emotional check-ins
- Receive supportive companion prompts while playing
- Save game records across devices with an account
- Review game history, move records, and emotion trends
- Use AI-assisted reports through a secure backend
- Adjust language, motion, contrast, hints, and sound settings

EmoChess is designed for learning and reflection. It is not a medical, diagnostic, or therapy service.
